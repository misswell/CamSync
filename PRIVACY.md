# CamSync Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated: July 27, 2026  
最后更新：2026 年 7 月 27 日

## English

CamSync is designed to transfer photos from folders and external storage authorized by the user to Apple Photos, local folders, or iCloud Drive.

### Data collection

CamSync does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection SDKs.

### Photos, files, and device history

- CamSync accesses only the photos, folders, and destinations that the user explicitly selects through Apple system interfaces.
- Photo and file contents are processed locally on the user's device and are not uploaded to CamSync servers.
- Sync history, source settings, transfer results, and file fingerprints are stored locally on the user's device to prevent accidental duplicate transfers and support interrupted transfers.
- Users can clear CamSync's locally stored sync history from within the app. This does not delete photos or files already saved at their destinations.
- If the user chooses iCloud Drive as a destination, Apple processes that data under the user's iCloud account and Apple's terms and privacy policy.

### Permissions

CamSync requests access to the photo library only to display albums and save photos to the destination selected by the user. Folder access is granted and managed through Apple's system file picker.

### Contact

For privacy questions or support, open an issue at <https://github.com/misswell/CamSync/issues>.

## 简体中文

CamSync 用于将用户授权的文件夹或外部存储中的照片传输到 Apple 照片、本机文件夹或 iCloud Drive。

### 数据收集

CamSync 不收集、不传输、不出售、也不共享个人数据。应用不包含广告、分析、跟踪或第三方数据收集 SDK。

### 照片、文件与设备历史

- CamSync 只访问用户通过 Apple 系统界面明确选择的照片、文件夹和保存位置。
- 照片和文件内容仅在用户设备本地处理，不会上传到 CamSync 服务器。
- 为避免意外重复传输并支持断点恢复，应用会在用户设备本地保存同步历史、来源设置、传输结果和文件指纹。
- 用户可以在应用内清除 CamSync 保存的本地同步历史；此操作不会删除已经保存到目标位置的照片或文件。
- 如果用户选择 iCloud Drive 作为保存位置，相关数据由 Apple 按照用户的 iCloud 账户、Apple 条款和隐私政策处理。

### 权限

CamSync 仅为显示相册列表以及将照片保存到用户选择的相册而请求照片图库权限。文件夹访问权限通过 Apple 系统文件选择器授予和管理。

### 联系方式

如有隐私或支持问题，请前往 <https://github.com/misswell/CamSync/issues> 提交问题。
